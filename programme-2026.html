About this edition

Mathematical Inception is a free, residential programme for students who have just completed Class 10. Rather than covering a lot of content, it helps students learn how to think: they work on carefully chosen problems, discuss ideas with one another and learn from mathematicians who explain their reasoning as they go.

The first edition was hosted and sponsored by Veveaham Schools, which made it free for every participant.

What students learned

The mathematics was built up in three stages over the week.

i.

Laying foundations

Mathematical statements, sets, functions, divisibility, counting and geometry.

ii.

Constructing mathematics

Using these foundations to prove selected theorems from Class 11 and 12.

iii.

Creating mathematics

Observing patterns, making conjectures and proving them independently.
