About this edition

Learning through discovery

Mathematical Inception is a free, residential summer workshop for students who have recently completed Class 10. Participants explore mathematical ideas, work through problems and practise explaining their reasoning.

The fourth edition ran for six days at Veveaham Schools, Dharapuram, which hosted and supported the workshop.

The programme

What participants do

i.

Laying foundations

Mathematical statements, sets, functions, divisibility, counting and geometry.

ii.

Constructing mathematics

Using these foundations to prove selected theorems from Class 11 and 12.

iii.

Creating mathematics

Observing patterns, making conjectures and proving them independently.
