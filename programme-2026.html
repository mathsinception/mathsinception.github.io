About this edition

Mathematical Inception is a free, residential workshop for students who have just completed Class 10. Rather than covering a lot of content, it helps students learn how to think: they work on carefully chosen problems, discuss ideas with one another and learn from mathematicians who explain their reasoning as they go.

The fourth edition was hosted and sponsored by Veveaham Schools, which made it free for every participant.

What students learned

The mathematics was built up in three stages over the week.

i.

Laying foundations

Mathematical statements, sets, functions, divisibility, counting and geometry.

ii.

Constructing mathematics

Using these foundations to prove selected theorems from Class 11 and 12.

iii.

Creating mathematics

Observing patterns, making conjectures and proving them independently.

The week

Each day had four teaching sessions and two discussion sessions, with sports or a cultural event in the evening.

Day 1 · Fri, 1 May
Inauguration. Sessions with Ananthnarayan H. and Arusha C. Evening sports.
Day 2 · Sat, 2 May
Sessions with Arusha C, Mohan R and Vignesh, and a guest lecture by C R Pradeep. Evening concert.
Day 3 · Sun, 3 May
Sessions with Vignesh, Omkar Javadekar and Vallmuthu. Evening sports.
Day 4 · Mon, 4 May
Sessions with Vallmuthu, Omkar Javadekar and Elanchearan R. S. Movie night.
Day 5 · Tue, 5 May
Sessions with Shailesh Goregaokar and Elanchearan R. S., and a guidance session on higher studies. Book reading.
Day 6 · Wed, 6 May
Final sessions with Elanchearan R. S. and Shailesh Goregaokar, then the valedictory function. Students travelled home in the afternoon.
