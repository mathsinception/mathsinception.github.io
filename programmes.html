About the programme

Mathematical Inception

Mathematical Inception is a free, residential summer workshop for students who have recently completed Class 10. Students explore mathematical ideas together, develop arguments and practise writing proofs.

The emphasis is on inquiry and understanding: asking questions, working through problems and experiencing the process of mathematical discovery.

Who
Students completing Class 10
How
Collaborative, residential learning
Cost
Free of charge
