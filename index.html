About the Trust

The Mathematical Inception Trust consists of people who believe that a vast country such as ours needs as many resources as possible to help spread scientific temper and critical thinking to each and every corner. This Trust has been established to play a small role in this effort, and owes an intellectual and philosophical debt to the Mathematical Training & Talent Search (MTTS) Programme, on which its current activities are modelled. While the MTTS activities are focussed at students at a higher level, our target audience so far has been high school students.

The Trust Objectives:

The objectives of the Trust include the following:

  1. Workshops and Experiential Programmes: To organize, conduct, and facilitate workshops, training sessions, interactive camps, and educational programmes for students, educators, and the community at large.
  2. Resource Creation (Learning & Human): To design, develop, publish, and disseminate physical and digital learning resources, curriculum materials, pedagogical tools, and to build human resources by training facilitators, mentors, and educators.
  3. Scientific Temper: To inculcate scientific temper, rational inquiry, observation-based learning, and a spirit of scientific curiosity among learners.
  4. Critical Thinking: To nurture critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and problem-solving capacities, enabling individuals to question, evaluate, and understand complex ideas independently.
  5. Mathematical Competence: To build confidence, conceptual clarity, and positive engagement with mathematics, removing fear and fostering aptitude for the subject.
  6. Independent Learning: To strengthen independent learning skills, self-directed exploration, and lifelong learning habits.
  7. Pedagogical Experimentation: To provide a dynamic platform, laboratory, and ecosystem for pedagogical experimentation, innovative teaching methodologies, and educational research.

The Trust aims to conduct activities keeping in mind the above objectives, in particular, conduct camps to help improve critical thinking in the participants and build their confidence, while removing the fear of mathematics through active engagement.

The activities of the Trust also endeavour to empower teachers by introducing them to pedagogically successful methods. An important outcome of organising camps as mentioned above, is the development of academic resources and pedagogical approaches for introducing mathematical ideas at the school level. Through this process, we aim not only to identify topics that can enrich the mathematics curriculum but also to develop effective ways of teaching them, making mathematical learning more engaging, intuitive, and exploratory.

Learning Philosophy:

The activities of the Trust adhere to our core belief of how we learn: through experimentation and exploration, alternated with introspection and discussion, and in particular, making mistakes, identifying them, and finding possible approaches to correcting them.

  1. One who has learnt how to learn will find it easier to pick up advanced content on their own. It is not our goal to introduce a lot of content, but to help the participants figure out how to learn. The resource persons think in front of the participants, ask questions, and look at well-chosen examples.
  2. Imitation is one of the natural methods of learning. The participants are encouraged to imitate this process and actively participate, both individually (through thinking) and with peers (through discussions). Active engagement makes learning a fun and fruitful activity.
  3. A wrong answer often offers a far better kick-start to the thinking process than a correct one. The participants are allowed to make mistakes. The resource persons help them identify those mistakes and consider potential ways to resolve them. Learning that mistakes are natural, and figuring out how to deal with them, also removes the fear of making mistakes.

What we are not!

One of the goals of the Trust is helping students learn through understanding, and in particular, learn how to learn. The activities of the Trust should not be confused with a coaching class; we are not actively helping prepare for entrance exams for elite institutes, nor teaching speed-techniques for competitive exams.

We believe that a confident student, who knows how to learn, can pick up those skills on their own.

The Trustees

Prior to the formation of the Trust, the Trustees had been involved with the Mathematical Inception camps each summer at Veveaham Schools in Dharapuram, since May 2023. In 2026, an MI camp was organised at the Poornaprajna Institute of Faculty Improvement, Bangalore, in collaboration with the Poornaprajna Group of Institutions. All the camps followed the above philosophy of learning. The feedback from the participants validated the approaches taken, and the success of these camps gave the Trustees the confidence in their methods, and led us to form the Mathematical Inception Trust in August 2026, to carry out further activities.
