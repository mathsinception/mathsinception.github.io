About MI Trust

The MI Trust was founded with the objective of carrying out activities that inculcate and nurture critical thinking while building mathematical confidence in students. The MI Trust was founded with the objective of carrying out activities that inculcate and nurture critical thinking while building mathematical confidence in school students. Even before its registration as a trust, the founding trustees had been conducting Mathematical Inception camps since May 2023.

What we do?

One of the main activities of the MI Trust is organising Mathematical Inception Camps for high school students. Since its inception in May 2023, the Trust has successfully conducted a camp every summer at Viveveham Schools in Dharapuram. In June 2026, an MI Camp was organised at the Poornaprajna Institute of Faculty Improvement, Bangalore, in collaboration with the Poornaprajna Group of Institutions.

An important outcome of organising these camps is the development of academic resources and pedagogical approaches for introducing mathematical ideas at the school level. Through this process, we aim not only to identify topics that can enrich the school mathematics curriculum but also to develop effective ways of teaching them, making mathematical learning more engaging, intuitive, and exploratory.
