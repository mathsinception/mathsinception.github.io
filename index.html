01 / About the trust

Who we are

Mathematical Inception Trust is a public charitable trust with a focus on mathematical learning. We want young people to experience mathematics as a subject they can question, investigate and enjoy.

Our aim is to nurture curiosity and independent thinking: to encourage students to look for patterns, explain their reasoning and learn alongside others.

02 / Our programmes

Mathematical Inception

A free, residential summer workshop for students who have just completed Class 10. The programme introduces students to mathematical thinking through collaborative problem-solving, writing proofs and guided exploration.
