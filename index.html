About the Trust

The Mathematical Inception Trust comprises of a group which believes that a large country such as ours needs as many resources as possible to help improve mathematical competence and critical thinking across its vast expanse. This Trust has been established to play a small role in this effort. It owes an intellectual and philosophical debt to the Mathematical Training & Talent Search (MTTS) Programme, on which its current activities are modelled. While the MTTS activities are focussed at students at a higher level, our target audience so far has been high school students.

The Trust Objectives

The objectives of the Trust include the following:

  1. To organise workshops and interactive camps which include training sessions, and educational programmes for students, educators, and the community at large.
  2. To provide a platform for pedagogical experimentation by creating an ecosystem for innovating and experimenting with teaching methodologies, with dynamic feedback from peers and experts.
  3. To create (learning & human) resources by training facilitators, mentors, and educators, and developing, publishing, and disseminating learning resources, curriculum materials, and pedagogical tools.
  4. To increase mathematical competence through activities which build confidence, conceptual clarity, and positive engagement with mathematics, removing fear and fostering aptitude for the subject.
  5. To strengthen independent learning by giving space for exploration and making mistakes, enabling individuals to question, evaluate, and understand complex ideas independently, and giving an opportunity to develop good learning habits via interaction with peers and experts.
  6. To nurture critical thinking and improve scientific temper by providing an environment fostering observation and inquiry, supporting analytical reasoning and problem-solving capacities, and inculcating a spirit of curiosity and teamwork via exchange of ideas with peers.

The Trust aims to conduct activities keeping in mind the above objectives, in particular, conduct camps to help improve critical thinking in the participants and build their confidence, while removing the fear of mathematics through active engagement.

The activities of the Trust also endeavour to empower teachers by introducing them to pedagogically successful methods. An important outcome of organising camps as mentioned above, is the development of academic resources and pedagogical approaches for introducing mathematical ideas at the school level. Through this process, we aim not only to identify topics that can enrich the mathematics curriculum but also to develop effective ways of teaching them, making mathematical learning more engaging, intuitive, and exploratory.

Learning Philosophy

The activities of the Trust adhere to our core belief of how we learn: through experimentation and exploration, alternated with introspection and discussion, and in particular, making mistakes, identifying them, and finding possible approaches to correcting them.

What we are not!

One of the goals of the Trust is helping students learn through understanding, and in particular, learn how to learn. The activities of the Trust should not be confused with a coaching class; we are not actively helping prepare for entrance exams for elite institutes, nor teaching speed-techniques for competitive exams.

We believe that a confident student, who knows how to learn, can pick up those skills on their own.

The Trustees

Prior to the formation of the Trust, the Trustees had been involved with the Mathematical Inception camps each summer since May 2023. All the camps followed the above philosophy of learning. The feedback from the participants, the success of these camps and the support they received gave the Trustees the confidence in their methods, and led them to form the Mathematical Inception Trust in August 2026, to carry out further activities.
