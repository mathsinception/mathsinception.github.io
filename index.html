01 / About MI

Who are we?

Mathematical Inception is an intense mathematics camp of 4-5 days of duration for highscool students and teachers. This camp aims to improve critical thinking in the participants and build their confidence, while removing the fear of mathematics through active engagement. It also endeavours to empower teachers by introducing them to pedagogically successful methods. We want young people to experience mathematics as a subject they can question, investigate and enjoy.

We are not a coaching class. This camp is not about preparing for competitive exams or speed techniques.

02 / Our programmes

What do we do (differently)?

It is not our goal to introduce a lot of content, but to help the participants figure out how to learn. The resource persons think in front of the participants, ask questions, and look at well-chosen examples. One who has learnt how to learn will find it easier to pick up advanced content on their own.

Imitation is one of the natural methods of learning. So, the participants are encouraged to do the same, actively participate in the process, both individually (thinking) and with peers (discussions). Active engagement makes learning a fun and fruitful activity.

A wrong answer often offers a far better kick-start to the thinking process than a correct one. The participants are allowed to make mistakes, the resource persons help them identify, and see potential ways of resolving the same.

03 / Expectation from participants

From asking to understanding

i.

Explore a question

Work with examples, notice patterns and make conjectures.

ii.

Think together

Discuss ideas, compare approaches and learn from one another.

iii.

Explain why

Move from an observation to an argument, and from an answer to a proof.
