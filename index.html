Who we are

The primary and subsidiary objects for which the Trust is founded are as follows:

  • Workshops and Experiential Programmes: To organize, conduct, and facilitate workshops, training sessions, interactive camps, and educational programmes for students, educators, and the community at large.
  • Resource Creation (Learning & Human): To design, develop, publish, and disseminate physical and digital learning resources, curriculum materials, pedagogical tools, and to build human resources by training facilitators, mentors, and educators.
  • Scientific Temper: To inculcate scientific temper, rational inquiry, observation-based learning, and a spirit of scientific curiosity among learners.
  • Critical Thinking: To nurture critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and problem-solving capacities, enabling individuals to question, evaluate, and understand complex ideas independently.
  • Mathematical Competence: To build confidence, conceptual clarity, and positive engagement with mathematics, removing fear and fostering aptitude for the subject.
  • Independent Learning: To strengthen independent learning skills, self-directed exploration, and lifelong learning habits.
  • Pedagogical Experimentation: To provide a dynamic platform, laboratory, and ecosystem for pedagogical experimentation, innovative teaching methodologies, and educational research.

What we do?

One of the main activities of the MI Trust is organising Mathematical Inception Camps for high school students. Since its inception in May 2023, the Trust has successfully conducted a camp every summer at Viveveham Schools in Dharapuram. In June 2026, an MI Camp was organised at the Poornaprajna Institute of Faculty Improvement, Bangalore, in collaboration with the Poornaprajna Group of Institutions.

An important outcome of organising these camps is the development of academic resources and pedagogical approaches for introducing mathematical ideas at the school level. Through this process, we aim not only to identify topics that can enrich the school mathematics curriculum but also to develop effective ways of teaching them, making mathematical learning more engaging, intuitive, and exploratory.
