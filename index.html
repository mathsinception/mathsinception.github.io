01 / About MI

Who are we?

Mathematical Inception is an intensive 4–5-day mathematics camp for high school students and teachers. The camp aims to strengthen participants’ critical-thinking skills and build their confidence, while helping them overcome their fear of mathematics through active engagement. It also endeavours to empower teachers by introducing effective teaching methods. We want young people to experience mathematics as a subject they can question, investigate, and enjoy.

We are not a coaching class. This camp is not about preparing for competitive exams or speed techniques.

02 / Our programmes

What do we do differently?

Our goal is not to introduce a lot of content, but to help participants learn how to learn. The resource persons model their thinking, ask questions, and explore carefully chosen examples. Those who learn how to learn can more easily explore advanced content independently.

Imitation is a natural way to learn. Participants are encouraged to imitate this process by engaging actively—thinking individually and discussing ideas with peers. Active engagement makes learning enjoyable and fruitful.

A wrong answer can be a better starting point for thinking than a correct one. Participants are encouraged to make mistakes; resource persons help them identify and explore possible ways to resolve them.

03 / Expectations of participants

From asking to understanding

i.

Explore a question

Work with examples, notice patterns and make conjectures.

ii.

Think together

Discuss ideas, compare approaches and learn from one another.

iii.

Explain why

Move from an observation to an argument, and from an answer to a proof.
